# Product: The B2B AI Standard for LinkedIn® Ads

- **What:** a free, public set of rules, skills and scenarios for running LinkedIn® Ads with AI, built with named practitioners and maintained by DemandSense. A *resource*, not a product.
- **Audience:** B2B LinkedIn Ads practitioners, agency and in-house, from business owners to Campaign Manager specialists.
- **Job of the page:** get practitioners to send a skill or a scenario before submissions close (21 Oct 2026); launch on stage at Full Circle (4 Nov 2026).
- **Mode:** Persuade. One action: "Bring your work" (mailto znak@demandsense.com).
- **Tone:** factual, generous, practitioner-first. Credit and recognition over selling. DemandSense appears as sponsor and optional MCP, never as the hero.
- **Content source:** https://the-ai-standard.vercel.app/ verbatim. **Look:** `DESIGN.md` (DS Report language).
